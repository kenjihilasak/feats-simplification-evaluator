# MEANING graded levels: blinded review

For each case, compare **Variant** with **Clean** and mark how much meaning the Variant has lost.
`None` = same meaning; `Some` = a minor detail lost; `A lot` = a central fact or clause lost or made vague; `Contradiction` = the Variant states something false; `Broken` = ungrammatical or nonsensical.
